I write software, lately mostly in Python, Go and JavaScript, and I have a weakness for ideas small enough to finish before Monday.

Some of them turn out useful. One is a ramen shop for yokai. One is a viking that sets fire to web pages. All of them taught me something, which is my official excuse.

The catalog keeps the lot: what each one is, what was fun or fiddly about building it, and a live page to poke at where there is one.
